# Serevin Support Operations 2.2

Serevin Support is a fictional service desk process created only for the Agent Factory validation experiment.

## Current contract

- Incident priorities are P0, P1, P2, and P3.
- A P0 incident requires acknowledgement within 10 minutes.
- A P1 incident requires acknowledgement within 45 minutes.
- P0 customer updates must be sent every 30 minutes.
- Two consecutive missed customer updates trigger escalation to the duty manager.
- Incident records are retained for 180 days.

## Legacy notes

The former policy allowed 15 minutes for P0 acknowledgement and retained records for 90 days. Those values are obsolete and must not be presented as the current policy.
